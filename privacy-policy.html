Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Who I am

This website (gulfaraz.me) is a personal portfolio operated by Gulfaraz Arshad, based in Multan, Punjab, Pakistan.

What this site uses

This site does not run general-purpose analytics, but it does use the following third-party services:

  • CallGauge — a form-tracking service used on the contact form. If you accept cookies, CallGauge may set a cookie to track that the form was submitted and to help understand where site visitors come from. If you decline, this script is not loaded.
  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded directly from Google's servers, which means your browser makes a request to Google (including your IP address) to fetch the fonts.
  • Tailwind CSS (CDN) — a styling library loaded from a public CDN. It does not collect personal data.

Cookies

A cookie banner is shown on your first visit. Choosing "Accept" allows the CallGauge form-tracking script to load and set its cookie. Choosing "Decline" keeps that script from loading. Your choice is remembered in your browser and can be reset by clearing your browser's site data.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the information you enter (name, email, phone number, and message) is submitted so I can respond to your inquiry. It is not sold or shared with third parties beyond the tracking described above.

Your choices

You can decline the cookie banner at any time, or clear cookies through your browser settings. If you'd like any data you've submitted through the contact form deleted, email me at farazgul446@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the tools or services used on this site change, this page will be updated to reflect that.
